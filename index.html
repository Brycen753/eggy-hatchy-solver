Eggy Hatchy SolverA fan-made move advisor for DragonVale's Eggy Hatchy minigame

How to use
  1. Enter your board. Click a cell, type the dragon, press Enter. Tab first if you need to set a level or Twin. Then fill in both goals and press Start.
  2. Follow the best move. The arrow shows it; the card shows your chance to win. Prefer a different move? Click an egg to see every move it can make.
  3. Enter what hatched. After you combine in the game, pick the result from the list. It's sorted by how likely each dragon is.
  4. Answer the quick questions. When an egg leaves a cell, say whether a power-up appeared. After a roll, the solver says whether a redo is worth spending.
  5. Bank every egg to finish. The game only ends once the board is empty, even if you've already met both goals. Then press New game to set up your next board.

Win chances come from thousands of simulated games using the community's breeding odds. They are estimates, not guarantees.
